FAQ ✿

Good to know

Everything before you order ♥

How do I place an order?
Tap "Add to order" on the menu to pick your flavours and quantities, then tap the order button at the bottom — it builds an itemised order you can send straight to us on WhatsApp. You are also welcome to just DM us on Instagram ♥
How long does it take?
Usually 2–3 days from order to pickup. For farewell boxes or large orders please book 1 week ahead. Rush orders — DM us and we will do our best ✿
Where do we meet?
Any purchase can be picked up at Fo Tan MTR Station. Over $150 we can meet at any station along the East Rail Line, and over $1000 we deliver to your door for free. SF Express (freight collect) is also available.
How long do they keep?
Mini brookies in a jar are best within 5 days and keep up to a week. Large brookies taste best the same day. Marshmallow ones are lovely with a quick microwave warm-up ✿
How do farewell boxes work?
Medium brookies at $25 each, minimum 20 pieces, 5% off over $1000. We can swap in a "thank you" sticker — please DM us 1 week ahead ♥
